Yoob TTS — pronunciation fixes: W1 text normalization & W2 lexicon

Same script, same voice, same seed. Rendered through the production request path (POST /v1/audio/speech). W1 before = raw text with normalization off (normalize:false); W2 before = plain spelling. Scored by the phone gate (wav2vec2-lv-60-espeak-cv-ft forced alignment of the target word span against its CMUdict/espeak readings; a take passes at PER ≤ 0.20).

The fixes being demonstrated (all server-side, no model retrain): W1 — text normalization frontendW2 — pronunciation lexicon
  • W1: serving/tts/textnorm.py rewrites symbols the grapheme-only models cannot read — ISO dates, currency, emails, URLs — into speakable words before the model ever sees them. Applied on both the 4B and the 192M serving lanes.
  • W2: serving/tts/lexicon/lexicon_v1.yaml, applied by textnorm.normalize() when TTS_LEXICON is set. Measured respellings for words whose spellings mislead the models; only entries that measured clean on both models are shown, the rest stay draft.

Honest accounting: of the 24 W2 probe words, several v1 respellings did not survive measurement on both models (sean→“shoo”, nasa→“nay-jer” on the 4B, colonel→“kænəl”, turquoise, gauge, sergeant, regatta worse; and epitome — its original “PER 0.00” was an artifact of scoring the respelling against itself; re-scored against the true /ɪpɪtəmi/, all corrected variants land PER 0.25–0.57) — exactly why the lexicon gates entries as draft until they pass, and why respellings are mined per model, not assumed. The four rows above are the entries that cleared the gate on both engines; the failing ones stay draft in lexicon_v1.yaml and are re-mined. This is the same measured-entry path the 4B/192M dictionaries use, generalized to any word list — no retraining, no GPU at serving time.
