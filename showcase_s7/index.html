Word-recall gate (new, S7.11b). The earlier take of joy_woman dropped the final “said” — the owner heard “she actually yes”, and granite, Qwen and the CTC arbiter all confirmed the word was missing while every audio-domain guard passed. Every render on this page now passes a word-recall gate: the CTC arbiter (wav2vec2-base-960h, greedy — the one decoder that cannot normalise a dropped word away) scores each window against its text and any window with a deletion is re-rolled (seed+7, up to 3 attempts), plus a clip-level pass on the joined audio.
