One model. Many people.

Every clip below is a single checkpoint reading a different line in a different voice. Nothing is cloned from a target speaker and nothing is hand-picked per clip — the same operating point renders all of them.

checkpoint

Seven checkpoints, the same 20 prompts and the same operating point (text/caption CFG 8.0, caption CFG 8.0, 32 steps, sway -1.0, best-of-4, seed 20260808). S7 is the shipped 32-dim JP32 model. S7.1 is the experimental 60k checkpoint trained from scratch on the exact unchanged S7 English corpus with JP32 latents. S7.2 is the Meta128 successor trained with prose captions (120k steps, warm-started from S7.1 step-60k). S7.3 is S7.2 retrained with gender-corrected prose captions (120k steps, best checkpoint at step 110K, val loss 1.5778). S7.4 is the JP32 model retrained with gender-corrected prose captions (120k steps, best checkpoint at step 106K, val loss 1.5622). S7.5 is S7.4 retrained with Tier 2 prose captions — per-source tempo reclassification, intensity/arousal, and silence modeling (120k steps, warm-started from S7.4 step-106K, best checkpoint at step 108K, val loss 1.5601). Speaker CFG 5.0. S7.5-stream is S7.5 fine-tuned for streaming with chunk-causal attention (chunk=8, lookahead=1, 75/25 offline/chunk mask mixture, 30k steps, LR 3e-5, best checkpoint at step 9K, val loss 1.5573). All seven use the English text tower.
