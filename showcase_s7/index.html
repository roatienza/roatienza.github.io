S7.6 is S7.5-stream-v2 retrained for full streaming — 0% offline, 100% chunk-causal masks with a sliding-window loss (clean past, noised current chunk, pure-noise future), discrete 8-step timesteps matching the streaming Euler schedule, 48-frame left-context bounding, and exposure-bias noise on past frames (50k steps, warm-started from S7.5-stream-v2 step-37K, best checkpoint at step 40K, cc_loss 0.8924). It is the first fully streaming checkpoint with no ASR regression against the offline-capable S7.5-stream-v2, fixing the v5 word-repetition regressions; at the 8-step incremental decode it reaches 174ms time-to-first-audio (3.2× faster than offline).
