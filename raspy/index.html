Raspy excited speech in yoob-tts-1 - what the markup controls

Four voices × six delivery variants × three takes · quality best · 48 kHz WAV · measured 2026-11-10 · HNR = Praat harmonicity, mean of 3 takes (higher = cleaner voice)

Emotion tags and shouting spans make the model perform strain - the same creak and subharmonics a real shouting voice has - which reads as rasp. The two structures behave differently, and the difference is consistent across all four voices: the shouting span is the harshest structure on the two breathy female voices (1.78 / 3.34 dB), while the plain excited tag is the cleanest way to get loud on the two male voices (5.02 / 5.77 dB). pick="expressive" is the wrong lever on the voices where polish is the problem: on w1243 it turns the cleanest excited structure (3.35 dB) into the second raspiest take of the whole set. Delivery instructions (clean voice, no rasp, no strain) are the one lever that flips per voice: they pull w1243's excited line up (3.35 → 3.23 dB mean, and the best instructed take is the cleanest excited clip of the four voices), but they cost the male voices clarity (m0886 5.77 → 4.31 dB) - the instructions override the voice's own bright, forward placement. The shout sound tag trades intensity for cleanliness everywhere: never the cleanest, never the worst, and the only loud marker that never drops a voice below 2.9 dB.
