How to score (Mean Opinion Score)

Each item below is the same sentence spoken by 7 anonymized systems (labeled System A–G). Listen to each version and rate its overall speech quality — naturalness of the voice, pronunciation and prosody, and freedom from artifacts (noise, glitches, robotic tone).

5Excellent — no perceptible flaw; sounds completely natural. 4Good — flaws are perceptible but not annoying; mostly natural. 3Fair — slightly annoying; noticeably unnatural at times. 2Poor — annoying; clearly unnatural. 1Bad — very annoying; hard to listen to.

To keep the test short, a random sample of 10 sentences (drawn from the 100-utterance validation holdout, seed 20260914) is split into 2 blocks of 5. Each rater is assigned one block and rates all 7 systems on its 5 sentences — 35 ratings, about 8 minutes. Blocks rotate so each gets equal coverage. The full 100-utterance machine composite for every system is shown in the statistics view.

  • Use headphones in a quiet room if possible.
  • Each sentence starts with the ground-truth reference (the original LJSpeech recording) — listen to it first, then compare each system against it.
  • Play each sample fully (at least once) before rating it — the score buttons unlock after playback starts.
  • You may replay any sample as often as you like before deciding.
  • Rate what you hear, not what you think it might be — the system identities are hidden on purpose.
  • Rate all 7 versions of a sentence, then press Submit ratings. Your scores are saved anonymously; no personal data is collected.
