How to score (Mean Opinion Score)

Each item below is the same sentence spoken by 8 anonymized systems (labeled System A–H), one of which may be a human recording. Listen to each version and rate its overall speech quality — naturalness of the voice, pronunciation and prosody, and freedom from artifacts (noise, glitches, robotic tone).

5Excellent — no perceptible flaw; sounds completely natural. 4Good — flaws are perceptible but not annoying; mostly natural. 3Fair — slightly annoying; noticeably unnatural at times. 2Poor — annoying; clearly unnatural. 1Bad — very annoying; hard to listen to.

To keep the test short, the 30 sentences are split into 6 blocks of 5. Each rater is assigned one block and rates all 8 systems on its 5 sentences — 40 ratings, about 10 minutes. Across raters, every system is still compared on every sentence, and blocks rotate so each gets equal coverage.

  • Use headphones in a quiet room if possible.
  • Play each sample fully (at least once) before rating it — the score buttons unlock after playback starts.
  • You may replay any sample as often as you like before deciding.
  • Rate what you hear, not what you think it might be — the system identities are hidden on purpose.
  • Rate all 8 versions of a sentence, then press Submit ratings. Your scores are saved anonymously; no personal data is collected.
