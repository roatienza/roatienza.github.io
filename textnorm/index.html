How disambiguation works

Unambiguous abbreviations (Mr., etc., API) are expanded directly from a ~360-entry dictionary organized by field: everyday & formal writing, texting & social media, tech, business, addresses, medicine, and more. Ambiguous ones carry a context rule — for example St. consults the words before and after it, HR looks for medical vs. office vocabulary in a window around the match, and IN must be all-caps and precede a ZIP or city before it becomes Indiana (so “In 2026” survives). The dictionary also accepts an optional LLM disambiguator as a fallback for cases the rules cannot decide.
